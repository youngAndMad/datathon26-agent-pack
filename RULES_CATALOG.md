## Activation gates

For every candidate, a local owner must:

1. Save the relevant official document and operative amendments, verify edition/scope, record the file hash and exact paragraph. Do not accept a similarly named unofficial domain as the authority.
2. Write positive, negative and unknown predicates plus disqualifying exceptions. Keep nonvisual prerequisites explicit.
3. Define full temporal opportunity and visibility requirements, not arbitrary “inspect for three seconds” rules.
4. Review authorized local examples and compliant counterexamples. If evidence is insufficient, keep disabled.
5. Write a limited Russian type and explanation template, with the exact source metadata included deterministically.
6. Run rule/evidence tests and record approval owner, date, limitations and registry version.

Do not require identification of a person to establish a role. Use scene interaction only when sufficient. Do not infer whether a person is authorized from appearance, clothing or a face. Legal inspection of identity documents is distinct from prohibited automated face/person identification.

## Excluded conclusions unless separately supported

Accounting entries, balances, posting correctness, reconciliation, entitlement, counterfeit status and exact cash discrepancies generally require invisible information. Paper/document absence does not prove a missing check because digital alternatives may exist. A hand entering a pocket is not automatically theft. A drawer looking closed does not prove a lock. Two visible collectors do not prove an incomplete crew. PPE provisioning language alone does not establish a universal indoor helmet-wearing duty.

The bank-premises security act, НБРК №14 of 24 February 2020, https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2000020075, is an additional research lead. Its operative detailed clauses were not sufficiently verified here; do not create a generic “door open” detector from its title.

Do not add phone, glove, posture, politeness, money-on-floor or screen-lock violations from intuition. A future rule needs the same source, applicability and evidence gates as every seed above.
