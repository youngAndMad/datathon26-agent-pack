## Method
- Text came only from the official endpoint `https://adilet.zan.kz/api/documents/by-ngr/<NGR>?language=rus`.
  - Fetch and derivation used the same code as `cashwatch.rules.capture` (`fetch`, `html_to_text`).
- Acts were located with the official full-text search at `old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/search/docs/fulltext=…`. A secondary news site (wfin.kz) was used only as a lead to the АРРФР instruction.
- Status of other candidates was checked through the official API:
  - V1200008080 (НБРК №250, 2012) — `yts`, repealed, end 23.02.2020, replaced by №14.
  - V070004817_ (2007) — `yts`.
  - V990001017_ — `yts`.
  - V010001482_ — `yts`.
  - V2000021466 — operative, but it covers only the buildings of the Национальный Банк itself, not second-tier banks. Not applicable.
  - P2100000305 (Government Decree №305, general anti-terrorism requirements) — operative. It is the umbrella act that L06 details. Not captured.
- Effective dates were computed from each act's own commencement clause plus the official «publication_reference» (ЭКБ date). The API field `publication_date` tracks the registration date, not the ЭКБ date, and was not used.
  - The rule is «по истечении десяти календарных дней после дня … первого официального опубликования»: days 1–10 start the day after publication, and the act is in force on the 11th day.
  - №71: ЭКБ 20.11.2025 → **01.12.2025**. The p.12 amendment is in п.4 of the Перечень. The exception in п.4 of the decree covers only п.3 of the Перечень (an amendment to №176), so the general term applies.
  - №88: ЭКБ 05.01.2025 → 16.01.2025. Its п.5 suspends the new wording of pp.11, 13(3), 14 and 16 of №14 until 01.01.2027. The suspension-period wording (what the snapshot shows) applies today.
  - АРРФР №21: ЭКБ 14.05.2024 → 25.05.2024.
- C, no later change to п.12:
  - The L01 consolidated text (database as of 14.10.2026) has only the №71 footnote for п.12.
  - L04 (№25/2026) does not amend п.12 of №231. Its «пункт 12-1» belongs to №176.

## Conclusions and recommendations
- **B: another person enters the касса or approaches the cash desk.**
  - **No official clause forbids** an employee or another person from entering the касса or approaching the cashier's desk during cash operations.
  - What exists:
    - L05 pp.8 and 16(1): structural only (isolated кассовый узел, a lockable entrance).
    - L01 п.75: limits entry to the **кладовая / сейфовая комната** only, not the касса.
    - L06 pp.13–15: a bank-defined пропускной режим and restricted-access zones. It applies only to РКО with capacity of 200 or more people. Who is authorised is set by the bank's internal act, so it cannot be checked from video.
  - Recommendation: **no rule for the касса/desk (no basis)**.
  - Optional narrow candidate: «посторонний в кладовой/сейфовой комнате» under L01 п.75. Even then it stays `unverified`, because authorisation and escort cannot be seen. Not recommended for enabling.
- **E: operations with a safe or темпокасса.**
  - **No official clause** requires a teller safe or темпокасса to be kept closed or locked.
  - **No official clause** requires dual control ("two people") when opening it. L01 п.2(16) «не менее двух работников» is the number of appointed responsible persons, not a presence requirement.
  - Key handling (L01 п.80) is left to internal documents.
  - What remains:
    - L01 п.20 ч.2: previously accepted cash belongs in lockable devices.
    - L01 п.42: a controller must be present for counting, sorting and packing outside client service (already CW08).
  - Recommendation: **no new rule**. At most, extend CW02 (п.20 ч.2) to «ранее принятые наличные вне запираемого устройства», which stays disabled until applicability is validated.
- **A: cashier leaves with no client present but cash on the desk.**
  - п.22 needs a client being served.
  - There is **no clause** on leaving cash unattended or locking the workplace when leaving.
  - The only hook is L01 п.20 ч.2 (only the current client's cash may be on the desk; earlier cash goes in lockable devices). It is indirect: the paragraph sits in the cash-acceptance section, and whose cash it is cannot be seen.
  - Recommendation: **do not enable a «покинул место без клиента» rule on п.22**. If anything, treat it as a CW02 (п.20 ч.2) case «наличные на столе вне обслуживания клиента», still disabled pending validation.
- **C: п.12.**
  - The current wording is confirmed: it is identical to the №71 amendment, in force since 01.12.2025, with no later change in the database as of 14.10.2026.
  - Recommendation: CW04 may cite **L01 п.12 ч.1–2 (ред. №71, в силе с 01.12.2025)**.
  - Remaining open question: does the teller workplace fall inside the кассовый узел (L01 п.5; L05 п.2(3) and п.7, where the касса is part of the кассовый узел)? This supports applicability when the cashier works inside a касса, and is unclear for open-desk service.
